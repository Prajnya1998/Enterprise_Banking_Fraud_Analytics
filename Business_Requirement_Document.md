# Business Requirement Document (BRD)

## Project Name
Enterprise Banking Fraud Analytics Platform

---

## Business Problem

ABC Bank has experienced an increase in fraudulent credit card transactions.
The management team wants to analyze transaction data to identify fraud patterns,
high-risk customers, and business insights that can help reduce financial losses.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze credit card transaction data.
- Identify fraudulent transactions.
- Discover fraud trends and patterns.
- Build KPIs for business users.
- Create an executive dashboard.
- Automate data processing using Python.
- Deliver actionable business recommendations.

---

## Stakeholders

- Chief Risk Officer

- Data Analytics Team
- Senior Management
